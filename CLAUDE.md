## Notes beyond AGENTS.md

- The site is just `README.md` rendered by GitHub Pages; there is nothing to build, lint, or test. Pushing to `main` deploys.
- Content conventions in `README.md` to keep when editing:
  - Each job is `### Role — Company` (em dash), followed by a bold date line (`**Mon YYYY – Present**`, en dash), a short description, then project bullets starting with `**Project Name:**`.
  - Per-project tech lines are indented continuation lines (`  **Tech:** ...`) under the bullet; job-wide tech/server lines (`**Tech:**`, `**Server:**`) sit after the bullet list. No trailing-space line breaks are used.
  - Sections are separated by `---` horizontal rules.
  - Hard Skills levels are 10-character bars of `█` and `░` followed by a label (Intermediate / Proficient / Advanced). Keep table columns padded and aligned.
  - Other Technologies is a single line of space-separated backticked items.
- Newest experience goes first.
